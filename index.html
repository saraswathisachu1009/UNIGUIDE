🛡️ How to Use Safely

  • Hold correctly: Grip the stick firmly near the handle
  • Scan ahead: Move the stick side-to-side to detect obstacles
  • Listen to alerts: Pay attention to buzzer patterns
  • Test in safe areas: Practice before using in unfamiliar places
  • Check surroundings: Don't rely solely on the device
  • Maintain device: Keep sensor clean and battery charged

🎨 Color Codes & Alerts

Green (Safe Zone)

Distance: > 100 cm

Status: Clear ahead - no immediate obstacles

Sound: Silent

Yellow (Caution Zone)

Distance: 50-100 cm

Status: Obstacle detected - proceed carefully

Sound: Slow beeping (every 600ms)

Red (Danger Zone)

Distance: < 50 cm

Status: Obstacle very close - stop immediately

Sound: Fast beeping (every 200ms)
