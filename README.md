Project Description
A high end note taking app equiped with the tabs of personal, business and real time obstacles detection helpful in visually impaired and other individual. A multipurpose app which has the ability to take measurement and sense obstacles actively displaying it.

The Problem statement
The obstacle detection in visually impaired people and also for calculating the traffic in real time for bussiness profeesional. it also has an in built notes and reminder system for busy individuals.

The Solution
An ultrasonic sensor at the end of the cane or the sensor connected to the vehicle of individuals and detect, help,record traffic which track the daily traffic in certain roads and alerts the individual on the day end. It also equiped with highly informative notes taking framework.

Components Required

Active 2-pin Buzzer

Jumper Wires – Male-to-Male, 5–10 pieces

Circuit Setup
HC-SR04 Pin	Connect To Arduino Pin
VCC	5V
GND	GND
TRIG	9
ECHO	10

Step 2: Buzzer Wiring
Buzzer Pin	Connect To Arduino Pin
+ (long)	6
– (short)	GND
Step 3: Power Supply (Battery)
Option 1 (9V Battery): Connect 9V battery to Arduino via connector
Red → VIN
Black → GND
